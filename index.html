01 — The gym

Built from bare concrete.
Run like it’s ours.

Infinity started as an empty shell in Kesar Tower. The team laid the flooring, hung the lights and bolted in every rack themselves — you can watch it happen further down this page.

Two years on, it’s a full floor of strength, functional and cardio kit under strip lighting, with a glass-walled studio for group classes and a window row that looks out over the city. The equipment keeps getting upgraded; the attitude stays the same.
